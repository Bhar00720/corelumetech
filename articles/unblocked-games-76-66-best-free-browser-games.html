How to Play Safely on School and Work Networks

While playing browser games during legitimate breaks is a great way to recharge mental focus, always keep these golden safety rules in mind:

  1. Never Download Shady .EXE or Chrome Extensions: Legitimate unblocked sites never force you to install desktop executable files or third-party browser extensions. If a page prompts you to download a "video player" or "Flash update", close it immediately.
  2. Avoid Untrusted Free VPNs: Installing unverified free VPN extensions on school-issued hardware frequently violates acceptable use policies and can expose your browsing traffic to telemetry data brokers. Stick to pure HTTPS browser URLs.
  3. Use Incognito or Clear Cookies Periodically: If you share a workstation, closing your private browser tab prevents your saved high scores from cluttering public profiles.
  4. Master Quick Boss Keys: When class or meetings resume, tap Ctrl + W (or Cmd + W on Mac) to instantly shut the tab without leaving audio playing in the background.
