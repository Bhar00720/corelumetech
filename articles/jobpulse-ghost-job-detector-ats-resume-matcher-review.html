Why Do Companies Post "Ghost Jobs" in 2026?

Understanding why ghost jobs exist reveals why human intuition alone cannot spot them:

  • Placating Burned-Out Employees: When departments are short-staffed, management often posts open requisitions to signal to overworked employees that "help is on the way"—even if hiring budgets are completely frozen.
  • Projecting Growth to Investors: Public and venture-backed companies know that active job boards signal healthy expansion. Removing open roles can look like stagnation, so listings stay published.
  • Passive Talent Harvesting: Recruiters collect resumes for future quarters so that if a key engineer or director resigns unexpectedly, they already possess a repository of pre-screened talent.
  • Automated Evergreen Syndication: In many large enterprises, ATS software automatically renews listings every 30 days without human intervention.
🚨 The True Cost to Job Seekers: The average job seeker applies to 120+ postings before landing an offer. If 40% of those listings are phantom jobs, candidates waste roughly 50+ hours writing customized applications that never reach human eyes.

How JobPulse's ATS Keyword Scanner Saves Applications

Over 90% of Fortune 500 companies utilize Applicant Tracking Systems (such as Workday, Greenhouse, Taleo, or Lever) to parse incoming resumes. If your resume lacks the exact keyword syntax listed in the posting (for instance, having "React" instead of "React.js and Next.js"), human recruiters never see your application.

JobPulse eliminates this blind spot. In the sidebar HUD, it lists:

  1. Matched Core Skills: Highlights skills you have verified in green.
  2. Missing Critical Keywords: Displays high-weight keywords present in the job posting that are currently absent from your resume in amber or red.
  3. Instant Fix Suggestions: Shows you where and how to naturally weave missing terms into your bullet points before clicking "Easy Apply."

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What is a ghost job and why do companies post them?

A ghost job is an active job listing for a position a company has no immediate intention of filling. Companies post ghost jobs to project growth to investors, keep a passive talent pipeline on standby, or placate overworked employees by making it look like help is coming.

How does JobPulse detect ghost jobs on LinkedIn and Indeed?

JobPulse examines multiple data signals including original posting age, repost frequencies, company hiring headcount velocity, application-to-hire ratios, and recurring recruitment patterns to calculate a real-time Ghost Job Probability Score.

How does JobPulse calculate an ATS match score?

JobPulse scans the active job description for critical keywords, hard skills, certifications, and years of experience requirements, comparing them directly against your uploaded resume to highlight missing keywords and generate an ATS compatibility percentage.

Is JobPulse free on the Chrome Web Store?

Yes, JobPulse is free to install directly from the official Google Chrome Web Store and works seamlessly on LinkedIn and Indeed job boards.
