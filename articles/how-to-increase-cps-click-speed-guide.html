How to Increase Your CPS (2026): Jitter vs Butterfly vs Drag Clicking

Published on August 24, 2026 • Corelume Tech Gaming Labs • 11 min read

1. Why Clicks Per Second (CPS) Dictates PvP Gaming

In fast-paced multiplayer games like Minecraft (1.8 PvP, Bedwars, SkyWars), Roblox, and competitive shoot-'em-ups, your **CPS (Clicks Per Second)** is often the decisive factor between winning a bridge duel or getting knocked into the void. A higher click rate delivers less knockback, registers faster hit combos, and enables rapid block clutching.

While the average casual gamer clicks at **6 to 8 CPS** using standard index-finger clicking, advanced competitive players achieve anywhere between **12 to 30+ CPS** by mastering specialized physical techniques. 2. Breakdown of the 4 Main Clicking Techniques

Regular Clicking 6 - 8 CPS

How It Works: Normal clicking uses your index finger muscles to repeatedly press the left mouse button. High aim precision and zero strain, but capped at modest speeds.

Jitter Clicking 10 - 14 CPS

How It Works: Vibrating your entire forearm and wrist to generate rapid micro-twitches on the mouse switch. Delivers incredible burst speed, but harder to maintain smooth crosshair aim.

Butterfly Clicking 15 - 22 CPS

How It Works: Alternating your index and middle fingers on the same mouse button in a continuous rhythm. Maximizes double-clicks on mice with low debounce time.

Drag Clicking 25 - 35+ CPS

How It Works: Dragging a textured or taped finger across the mouse button to create friction vibrations that trigger dozens of rapid micro-switches. Best for godbridging.

4. Hardware Optimization: Debounce Time & Mouse Switches

Your hardware plays an enormous role in reaching top CPS tiers. Standard office mice feature a high "Debounce Time" (10ms–15ms) designed to eliminate accidental double clicks. However, competitive gaming mice allow you to lower debounce time down to 0ms–4ms in software, enabling two clicks per single finger tap on optical/mechanical switches (e.g. Glorious Model O, Bloody A70, Roccat Kone Pro).

You can check whether your mouse switches are functioning properly or suffering from chatter latency using 🖱️ ClickSpeedLab's Hardware Checker.

5. Daily Training Routine to Boost Your CPS

  1. Warm Up (2 minutes): Perform wrist rotations and finger stretches to promote blood flow and prevent repetitive strain.
  2. 5-Second Burst Drills (5 rounds): Take 5-second sprints on ClickSpeedLab focusing on maximum forearm vibration.
  3. 60-Second Stamina Tests (3 rounds): Practice maintaining consistent butterfly rhythm without slowing down.
  4. Aim + Click Integration: Jump into a browser game like War Tanks or Sky Dodge to practice tracking moving targets while maintaining high APM.
