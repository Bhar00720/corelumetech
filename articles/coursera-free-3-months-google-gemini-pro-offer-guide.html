How to Claim 3 Months of Google Gemini Pro / AI Premium Free via Coursera (2026 Guide)

Google and Coursera have partnered to offer an exceptional perk for students and working professionals: 3 free months of the Google One AI Premium Plan (ordinarily valued at \$19.99/month or ~\$60 total / ₹5,850 in India). This package unlocks full access to Gemini Advanced (powered by Google's latest Gemini 1.5 Pro engine), 2TB of cloud storage across Google Drive and Photos, and native Gemini AI integration inside Gmail, Google Docs, Slides, and Sheets.

However, social media forums and Reddit threads are filled with confused learners who enrolled in a course only to discover their promotion code was missing, failed during checkout, or gave an "account not eligible" error. To save you time and money, this guide provides the verified, step-by-step claiming process, exact eligibility rules, and a clear breakdown of every scenario where this offer cannot be claimed.

💡 Non-Misleading Disclosure: This promotion is an official benefit tied to paid enrollments in select Google courses on Coursera. You cannot claim this offer via free course auditing or financial aid waivers, and Google requires an active personal payment method to set up the 90-day trial without upfront charges.

What Exactly Is Included in the 3-Month Offer?

When you successfully redeem this perk, your Google Account is upgraded to the Google One AI Premium Plan for 90 days. Here is what you receive:

Included Feature Standard Value Details
Gemini Advanced (1.5 Pro) \$19.99 / mo 1,000,000+ token context window. Upload 1,500-page PDFs, complex code repos, and raw spreadsheets directly into the prompt.
2 TB Cloud Storage \$9.99 / mo Massive expanded cloud storage shared across Google Drive, Gmail, and original-quality Google Photos backups.
Gemini in Workspace Included Draft emails directly inside Gmail, write and polish essays in Google Docs, and generate slide presentations automatically.
Google One Benefits Included Extended family sharing (up to 5 members can share the 2TB storage), premium photo editing tools in Google Photos, and priority support.

Eligible Courses: Where Does the Offer Come From?

Coursera does not hand out Gemini Pro promo codes to every random account. The offer is officially bundled with specific Google certificate and AI literacy curriculums:

  • Google AI Essentials: A 9-to-10 hour foundational course taught by Google AI specialists covering prompt engineering, productivity workflows, and responsible AI use.
  • Google Prompting Essentials: Focused deep-dive into crafting multi-step prompts, system instructions, and chained reasoning.
  • Google Professional Certificates: Select career certificate tracks, including Google Data Analytics, Google Cybersecurity, Google IT Support, and Google Project Management (check the specific course enrollment page for the active Google One AI promotional badge).
  • Coursera Plus Subscribers: Learners with an active Coursera Plus monthly or annual subscription who enroll in an eligible Google program.

Step-by-Step: How to Claim Your 3 Months Free

Follow these steps precisely to ensure your code generates and activates smoothly:

Step 1

Enroll in a Participating Course (Paid Tier)

Log into Coursera and enroll in an eligible course (such as Google AI Essentials). Ensure you select the paid certificate option or access it via an active Coursera Plus subscription. Do not select "Audit Only."

Step 2

Navigate to Course Welcome / AI Module

Open the course syllabus from your Coursera dashboard. Within the first module (usually titled "Welcome to Google AI Essentials" or "Your Course Resources & AI Perks"), look for the designated reading section titled "Access Your 3-Month Google AI Pro Subscription".

Step 3

Generate Your Unique Google Promo Link

Click the custom promotional link provided in the reading module. This link contains a unique partner authorization token that routes you to the Google One subscription checkout page.

Step 4

Log in with an Eligible Personal Google Account

Sign in using your personal @gmail.com address (see eligibility restrictions below). The page should display: "3 months free, then \$19.99/month" (or your local currency equivalent).

Step 5

Enter Payment Method & Confirm (\$0 Initial Charge)

Add a valid credit card, debit card, or Google Pay payment method. Google performs a temporary \$0 or minimal identity pre-authorization that is immediately refunded. You will not be charged a monthly subscription fee for the first 90 days.

CRITICAL: When Can the Offer NOT Be Claimed?

To avoid frustration, understand the strict boundary conditions Google enforces. You will receive an error or fail to see the trial under the following circumstances:

⚠️ Reasons You Cannot Claim the Offer:
  • Free Course Audits & Financial Aid: If you enrolled in Coursera via the free "Audit Course" button or had 100% of your tuition waived via Coursera Financial Aid, you do not qualify. The perk requires a paying student or Coursera Plus subscriber status.
  • Google Workspace / School / Work Accounts: You cannot activate Google One on an organizational account (e.g., student@university.edu or name@company.com). It must be a standard personal @gmail.com account.
  • Existing Google One Subscribers: If you are already paying for an active Google One 100GB, 2TB, or AI Premium plan, Google's billing system does not allow promotional trial stacking. You must either wait for your current subscription to expire, downgrade first, or redeem with an alternative personal Google account.
  • Third-Party Billed Google One Plans: If your Google One plan is currently billed through a telecom carrier, mobile network bundle, or Apple App Store, the Coursera promo will fail.
  • Family Group Non-Managers: If you are part of a Google Family Group where another member is the Family Plan Manager, you cannot independently activate an individual AI Premium plan.
  • Prior Promotion Redemptions: If your specific Google account has already redeemed a Google One AI Premium trial in the past 12 months, Google's system will flag you as ineligible.
  • Age & Regional Restrictions: Gemini Advanced requires the account holder to be 18 years or older, and the Google One AI Premium plan must be officially supported in your country.

How to Avoid Surprise Charges: Cancelling Auto-Renewal

Because Google requires a payment method to start the 90-day trial, your subscription is configured to auto-renew at \$19.99/month (or your country's monthly rate) on day 91. If you only want the free 3 months, follow this cancellation routine:

  1. Set a Calendar Reminder: Mark your calendar for Day 85 (5 days before the 90-day trial concludes).
  2. Visit Google One Settings: Navigate to one.google.com/settings on any desktop or mobile browser.
  3. Cancel Membership: Click on "Change membership plan" → "Cancel membership".
  4. Confirm Retained Access: In most geographic jurisdictions, Google One permits you to continue enjoying your 3-month trial perks until the exact scheduled renewal date, even after cancelling auto-renewal. Your stored files remain secure, though your storage will return to the default 15GB once the trial officially ends.
✅ Pro Tip for Students: While using your 3-month trial, take advantage of the 1M+ token context window in Gemini Advanced by uploading entire textbooks, syllabus PDFs, and practice exams to generate interactive flashcards and customized coding exercises!

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Can I keep the course notes and certificates if I cancel Google One?

Yes. Your Coursera course progress, certificates, and learning credentials are fully independent of your Google One subscription. Cancelling Google One will have zero impact on your Coursera standing.

What happens to my Google Drive files if I exceed 15GB after the trial?

If you upload more than 15GB of files during your 2TB trial and do not renew, your existing files will not be deleted immediately. However, you will not be able to upload new files, sync new photos, or send/receive emails in Gmail until you free up space or resubscribe.

Can I use a debit card or virtual card?

Yes, as long as international online transactions are enabled and the card supports recurring automated billing for identity verification purposes.
